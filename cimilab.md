## About CIMILab

CIMILab is an interdisciplinary research lab advancing machine learning, computational intelligence, and robust AI systems for high-impact scientific and societal applications.
Our work spans fundamental methods, benchmark-driven evaluation, and deployable systems that support reliable decision-making in constrained and high-stakes environments.

## Research Pillars

- Machine Intelligence: Deep learning, representation learning, uncertainty-aware modeling, and explainable AI.
- Computational Informatics: Data-centric pipelines, multimodal fusion, optimization, and algorithmic efficiency.
- Trustworthy AI Systems: Robustness, generalization, calibration, reproducibility, and fairness-aware evaluation.
- Translational AI: End-to-end systems that bridge research prototypes to practical use in real-world workflows.

## Current Research Themes

- Medical and health AI for segmentation, prognosis, and decision support.
- Multimodal learning from heterogeneous data sources.
- Efficient AI for low-resource and edge-constrained settings.
- Human-centered AI with interpretability and human-in-the-loop feedback.
- Benchmarking and evaluation frameworks for reliable model comparison.

## Reproducibility Standard

CIMILab follows a reproducible research workflow:

- Versioned datasets and preprocessing pipelines.
- Fixed seeds and documented training settings.
- Reported ablations, error analysis, and uncertainty considerations.
- Clear model and data licensing for reuse.
- Scripted evaluation for repeatable leaderboard metrics.
